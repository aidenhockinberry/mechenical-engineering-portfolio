Gravity-Driven Escapement & Ratchet System

Designed and manufactured an entire gravity-powered pendulum clockwork mechanism. This comprehensive research study involved developing 14 discrete CAD iterations, 5 chain drive versions (from LEGO tread to custom modular 3D-printed link chains), and 3 ratchet generations to achieve continuous, deadbeat energy impulse transfer.

⚙️ Kinematic Sub-Assemblies
  • Escapement Wheel & Anchor: Optimized pallet tooth angle to minimize recoil and deliver consistent impulse to the pendulum bob.
  • Drive Train Evolution: Transitioned from LEGO treads (V1) to custom interlocking links (V5) for higher tensile strength without stretching.
🔧 The Iteration Graveyard
  • Documented dozens of discarded prints to analyze tooth wear, friction coefficients, and center-distance tolerances in horological engineering.

Brushed DC (BDC) Motor Prototype

Designed and assembled a functional brushed DC motor utilizing LEGO Technic for the structural chassis and a hand-wound copper wire armature. The project required micro-positioning of the commutator and brush system to ensure continuous current reversal and consistent torque generation.

⚙️ Electromechanical Highlights
  • Lorentz Force Rotor: Hand-wound magnetic coil armature situated inside a permanent magnetic field.
  • Stability Tower & Contacts: Spring-tensioned copper brush contacts aligned with commutator split rings.
🔧 Challenges & Iterations
  • Flux Density: Swapped initial wiring for high-conductivity enamel magnet wire to increase coil density.
  • Field Calibration: Tested multiple neodymium magnet array configurations to maximize RPM without stalling.

Biomimetic Robotics & Logic-Driven Kinematics

Designed a multi-legged walking mechanism integrating sequential rotation matrices and an on-board digital logic array to coordinate joint phase shifts. This mechatronic system synchronizes mechanical linkages to translate rotary motion into a stable linear gait while optimizing runtime timing to prevent joint binding.

⚙️ Digital Logic Architecture
  • Boolean Gate Matrix: Configured standard logic gates (AND, OR, XOR, NAND) to handle state machines and control feedback loops.
  • Phase Shifting: Prevents opposing leg pairs from entering identical stride phases simultaneously.
🔧 Sequential Multi-Axis Actuation
  • Functions exactly like a real-world programmable microcontroller (e.g. Arduino) to time specific rotational angles of hip and knee joints during walking cycles.

Linear Transport System Prototype

Designed a heavy-duty linear transport carriage utilizing a dual-motor synchronized drive to balance load distribution across track guide beams. Focused on structural rigidity during high-cycle dynamic reversals.

⚙️ Key Technical Features
  • Dual Motor Drive System: Synchronized high-torque motors for balanced power distribution and reduced belt slippage.
  • Reinforced Chassis Design: Robust Technic beam structure designed to minimize vibration during high-cycle load reversals.
🔧 Challenges & Solutions
  • Challenge: Initial belt tension issues causing dynamic instability and tooth jumping.
  • Solution: Iterated pulley and track configurations to optimize traction and alignment under varying loads.
  • Inertia Management: Implemented structural cross-bracing and a tuned gear-train to absorb kinetic energy.

Self-Stabilizing Dynamic Rolling Structure

Conceived and engineered a large-scale self-stabilizing rolling ring structure. By distributing radial structural truss members symmetrically around a central hub, the mechanism maintains rotational momentum and self-rights under dynamic perturbation.

⚙️ Structural Dynamics
  • Radial Truss Geometry: Multi-point radial spoke network providing high torsional stiffness against lateral roll forces.
  • Gyroscopic Tendency: Perimeter mass concentration maximizing moment of inertia ($I = m r^2$).
🔧 Digital-Physical Bridge
  • Modeled in 3D wireframe space to verify angular symmetry before physical assembly with modular structural struts.
